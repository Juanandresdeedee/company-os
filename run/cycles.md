# Cycles

## Operating cycles

| Cycle | purpose | cadence | inputs | outputs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term | Purpose, vision, strategic ambition | 12 to 36 months | Company context, goal, thesis | Strategic direction |
| Annual | Strategy refresh and major bets | Yearly | Results, market, thesis | Annual priorities |
| Quarterly | Project and process steering | Quarterly | process definitions, projects, risks | Focus and resource choices |
| Monthly | Process and risk review | Monthly | Process metrics, project status | Adjustments |
| Weekly | Execution rhythm | Weekly | Current work, blockers | Decisions and next actions |

## Feedback loop

Process metrics and project learnings become inputs for analysis. Accepted analysis can update today, thesis, tradeoffs, processes and projects.
