# Positioning

## What we are

Describe the company plainly.

## What we are not

List categories, labels or assumptions that are incorrect.

## Current stage

Describe company size, model, maturity and constraints.

## Category

What category are we in, creating, or reframing?

## Core promise

What outcome do we promise?

## Differentiation

Why can we credibly win?

## Alternatives

What do customers compare us against?

## Validation Register

### Accepted claims

| Claim | active-use rule |
| --- | --- |
| State the accepted claim | Define where and how it is used as brand law |

### Hypotheses under validation

| Hypothesis | validation needed | use until evidence lands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe the claim to test | Name the evidence that would validate or falsify it | State whether it may be used internally, tested in limited external copy, or must not be presented as fact |

Do not let a locked positioning document silently promote a hypothesis or legacy artifact into accepted brand law.
