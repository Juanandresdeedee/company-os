# Market

`company/market/` contains durable market context.

Use it to separate market understanding from company identity.

## What belongs here

- market signals
- market intelligence
- competitor categories
- alternative solutions
- positioning evidence
- buying context

## What does not belong here

- live CRM state
- raw research dumps
- unreviewed notes
- generated campaign drafts
- client-confidential material

## Recommended files

- `intel.md`
- `competition.md`

## Prompts for operators

- What is changing in the market?
- Which alternatives do customers compare us with?
- Which competitors matter and why?
- What durable insight should agents know before writing or advising?
