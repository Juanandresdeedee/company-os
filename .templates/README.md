# Templates

`.templates/` contains reusable templates for durable Company OS documents.

## What belongs here

- document templates
- frontmatter examples
- reusable scaffolds for company, strategy, run, change, wiki, client, decision, and output files

## What does not belong here

- active company context
- generated drafts
- raw source material
- private credentials

## Rule

Templates should be generic and safe to copy. They should include required metadata and prompts, not company-specific claims.
